Our Commitment to Your Privacy
OneLine Atlas is built on a strict local-first, privacy-first principle. We believe your personal thoughts and memories belong exclusively to you.

1. No Data Collection
We do not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal data, journal entries, mood markers, tags, or usage analytics on external servers.

2. Local Storage Only
All journal entries are stored strictly and securely in local app storage on your device.

3. Zero Permissions Required
OneLine Atlas does not request or access your location, camera, photo gallery, contacts, microphone, or network state.

4. No Third-Party SDKs
We do not integrate ads, analytics trackers, or third-party SDKs of any kind.

5. User-Initiated Exports
Any export of your journal data is performed locally via Android's secure system file-saving interface (Storage Access Framework) only when explicitly triggered by you.

6. Complete Local Control
You can erase all local data anytime with a single tap from within the app.

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at neoacademic200@gmail.com
